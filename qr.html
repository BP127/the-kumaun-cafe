🏔️ Generate Menu QR

Create a QR code that customers can scan to open the digital menu of The Kumaun Cafe.

How to use:
  1. Host the kumaun-cafe folder on GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or any free static host.
  2. Copy the public URL of index.html and paste it above.
  3. Generate the QR and print it.
  4. Place the printed QR on every table.
  5. Customers scan → open menu → order → send to your WhatsApp.

Tip: You can also create table-specific URLs later (e.g. ?table=5) if you want.
